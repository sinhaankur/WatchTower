**Version 1.0 — effective 2026-06-12**

the Software, its users, and third parties out of harm's way. You agree
NOT to use the Software to:

1. **Break the law.** No use that violates any applicable law or
   regulation, including computer-misuse, privacy, intellectual-
   property, export-control, and sanctions laws.

2. **Attack systems you don't control.** The deployment, SSH, and
   provisioning tooling may only target machines and accounts you own
   or are explicitly authorized to manage. No unauthorized access, no
   scanning or exploiting third-party systems, no denial-of-service.

3. **Host or distribute harmful content.** No deploying malware,
   phishing infrastructure, command-and-control services, or content
   that is illegal in the jurisdictions where you operate.

4. **Infringe rights.** No deploying or distributing content that
   infringes copyright, trademark, trade secrets, or privacy or
   publicity rights of others.

5. **Abuse third-party services.** Respect the terms and rate limits of

   are yours.

6. **Misrepresent the Software.** Don't remove or falsify license
   notices, and don't present a modified installation as an official
   release of the Authors.

   (`pro/`) may not be offered to third parties as a managed service or

this policy with their users. Violations may expose you to liability;
the Authors accept none for prohibited use.
